It looks like you have a clean, modern foundation for electricalinstallers.com.au (it appears to be built using Replit, which is excellent for a lightweight, fast-loading site!). However, the electrical trade niche in Victoria—specifically covering the Mornington Peninsula, Bayside, and the South East Melbourne corridor—is highly competitive on Google.

To get your site onto the front page, you need to transition it from a sleek digital business card into a local SEO powerhouse.

Here is an actionable game plan, including how to leverage your .com.au presence to win local rankings.

1. Expand Beyond a Single-Page Layout
Right now, your site operates largely as a single-page layout. While this looks sharp, Google ranks individual pages, not just websites. It is incredibly difficult to rank on the front page for "3-Phase Upgrades" and "Electrician Mornington Peninsula" using the exact same URL.

Create Service Pages: Build dedicated landing pages for your high-margin services (e.g., /3-phase-upgrades, /underground-power-installation, /commercial-electrician).

Create Location Pages: Build geo-targeted pages for your main service hubs (e.g., /electrician-mornington-peninsula, /electrician-bayside). This allows you to tailor the text, headings, and meta tags to specific local searches.

2. Dominate the Google Map Pack (Local SEO)
For local trades in Australia, the "Map Pack" (the three map listings at the top of Google search results) is where the majority of clicks happen.

Optimize Your Google Business Profile (GBP): If you haven’t claimed it, do so immediately. Ensure your service areas are explicitly defined across the Mornington Peninsula and South East Melbourne.

Review Velocity: Actively ask your clients for 5-star Google reviews. When replying to them, naturally weave in local keywords (e.g., "Thanks John, glad we could help with your switchboard upgrade in Frankston!").

3. Sharp On-Page SEO & Keyword Targeting
Google needs explicit signals to understand what you do and where you do it within seconds of crawling your site.

H1 Headers: Your primary headline on the homepage shouldn't just say "Electrical Installers." Update it to a targeted H1 tag like: <h1>Licensed Electricians & Electrical Installers Mornington Peninsula</h1>.

Meta Descriptions: Write compelling snippets for Google search results that include your primary service locations, your phone number (0419 868 703), and a clear call-to-action (e.g., "Get a free quote today").

4. Maximize the .com.au Ecosystem
(Regarding your note on .com.au signals)
Google Australia (google.com.au) heavily rewards local digital footprints. Simply owning a .com.au domain isn't enough; you need to anchor it into the Australian web ecosystem.

Australian Directory Citations: Build foundational links on authoritative Australian trade directories like Hipages, ServiceSeeking, TrueLocal, Word of Mouth (WOMO), and Yellow Pages AU.

NAP Consistency: Ensure your Name, Address, and Phone number (NAP) are identical across your website, your GBP, and every external directory. Discrepancies confuse Google's algorithm.

Local Business Schema: Inject LocalBusiness structured data (Schema markup) into your site's code. Make sure to include your ABN inside this data—Google uses Australian Business Register data to verify legitimate, local operations.
